## Brush class

The brush.
 Brushes are used to paint the interior of the geometric shapes defined by a [Path](../path/) element
 and the characters rendered with a [Glyphs](../glyphs/) element.
 They are also used to define the alpha transparency mask in the [Canvas](../canvas/).OpacityMask,
 [Path](../path/).OpacityMask, and [Glyphs](../glyphs/).OpacityMask property elements.
